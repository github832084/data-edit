## What they share

1. **All are horizontal (16:9) YouTube videos of 3 to 29 minutes. None is a 9:16 reel.**
2. **No face on camera.** A narrator's voice over pictures. Our voice.md says "to-camera". This must be decided.
3. One line of subtitle at the bottom, often a serif font. Key word in gold or colour.
4. The verse is shown in a fixed "card": Devanagari, then transliteration, then meaning, then the source (book, chapter, verse). Seen in #2, #5, #8, #10, #12.
5. The title is a question or a story: "Why is…", "If only one verse…", "The slowest pupil".
6. The first line of the description is the hook again. Then the full verse, then the source.
7. Series: "EP 1", "Part 1", "Verse 4 of 14". People come back for the next one.
8. One look for the whole video, and a calm colour palette (black + gold, or cream paper).
